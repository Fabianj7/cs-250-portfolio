How do I interpret user needs and implement them into a program? How does creating user stories help with this? 

I interpret user needs by understanding what the user expects from the program and identifying the features needed to meet those expectations. Creating user stories helps break down requirements into smaller tasks that developers can understand and complete. User stories also help keep the development process focused on the user's needs. 

How do I approach developing programs? What Agile processes do I hope to incorporate into my future development work?

When developing programs, I prefer to break larger tasks into smaller steps so they are easier to manage. Agile practices such as sprint planning, regular communication, and retrospectives can help improve organization and teamwork. I hope to incorporate these practices into my future development projects to stay flexible and continuously improve my work. 

What does it mean to be a good team member in software development? 

Being a good team member means communicating effectively, completing responsibilities, and supporting others throughout the project. It is important to listen to feedback, share ideas, and adapt when changes occur. Through this course, I learned that teamwork and communication are just as important as technical skills when developing successful software.
